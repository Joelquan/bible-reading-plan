    Questions

    How long does it take to read the Bible in a year?

    About 3 chapters a day, roughly 15 to 20 minutes of reading. The Bible in a Year plan spreads all 1,189 chapters across 365 days.

    What is a chronological Bible reading plan?

    It arranges the books of the Bible in the order their events happened, so you read the story as it unfolded in history rather than in the traditional book order. Each book is still read straight through.

    Which Bible translation does this plan use?

    The plan follows the King James Version (KJV) chapter structure, which matches the chapter divisions used by almost all English Bible translations.

    Can I print my Bible reading plan?

    Yes. Build your plan above, then tap Print Plan for a clean printer-friendly checklist you can tick off by hand.

    How many hours does it take to listen to the whole Bible on audio?

    About 70 to 75 hours at normal speed. At 1.5x speed it takes about 47 to 50 hours, and at 2x speed about 35 to 38 hours. Use the Audio Bible plan above, pick your daily listening time and playback speed, and it will tell you your finish date.

    How many hours does it take to read the whole Bible?

    About 70 hours at a steady reading pace. Use the Reading by Time plan above, pick how long you read each day, and it will show how many days it takes and when you finish.
